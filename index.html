A few things you might wonder.

How can I remove ad banners while browsing in Chrome?

Install No Ads. Just Tasks. from the Chrome Web Store. It finds ad banners on the pages you visit and replaces them with colorful sticky notes, giving you space for tasks and reminders where those banners used to be.

Can I replace website ads with sticky notes?

Yes. No Ads. Just Tasks. turns the ad banners it finds into sticky notes. Click a note to add a task or reminder, then use its checkbox to mark the task as done.

What’s the difference between No Ads. Just Tasks. and a regular ad blocker?

A regular ad blocker focuses on blocking ads. No Ads. Just Tasks. focuses on turning advertising space into something useful: sticky notes for your tasks and ideas. It replaces the banners it finds rather than simply leaving their space empty; it does not promise to block every type of ad.

What is No Ads. Just Tasks.?

No Ads. Just Tasks. is a Chrome extension that replaces ad banners with colorful sticky notes for tasks and reminders.

How do I install it?

Click Add to Chrome on this page to open the official Chrome Web Store listing. Follow the installation steps in Chrome.

How do I add and complete a task?

Click a sticky note to write a task. Use its checkbox to mark the task as completed.

Will my tasks stay when I visit another page?

Yes. Your notes stay with you when you refresh a page or open another site, appearing in the next ad slot the extension replaces.

Can I organize and export my tasks?

Use colors for themes such as shopping, health, travel, and work. You can export open and completed tasks together in one file.

Does the extension support different languages?

The extension interface follows your browser language. This promotional website is in English.

Where can I read about privacy and report a problem?

Read the privacy policy for the developer’s published information about data handling. To report a bug or suggest a feature, email maxim4eg@gmail.com.
