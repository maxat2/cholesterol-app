Privacy Policy

No Ads. Just Tasks. (“the extension”) replaces ad banners with sticky-note to-do lists. This policy explains what data we handle.

Tasks you write

Your notes and tasks are saved in your browser with Chrome’s local storage. They stay on your device. We do not upload the text of your tasks or the websites you visit.

Usage analytics

To understand how many people use the extension, we send anonymous events to Yandex Metrica (counter 112779370), for example: install, browser start, popup open, stickies shown, export, uninstall.

These events may include a random client ID stored in your browser and technical data such as IP address, country/region, and browser type. They do not include your name, email, task text, or page URLs.

Permissions

storage — to keep your stickies between sessions.

Access to websites — to find ad blocks on pages you open and place stickies there. Page content is not sent to us.

Remote code

The extension does not load or execute remote JavaScript. All code ships inside the extension package.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email listed on the Chrome Web Store page.
